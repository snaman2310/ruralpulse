Photo rules & consent

Four rules get your photo approved the first time. Read the privacy part — it protects you and your neighbours.

Before you photograph anything
  • These photos and their GPS coordinates may be published in an open AI dataset. Anyone with the dataset can see exactly where the photo was taken.
  • Never photograph children, or a person's face, without that person's permission. Use the privacy flags on the capture screen and the reviewer will blur or refuse the photo.
  • Public buildings (schools, clinics, panchayat offices, wells) are fine. Private homes are not — ask the owner first, or flag it as a private property.
  • You stay responsible for consent. A photo published without permission can be taken down and the reward reversed.
